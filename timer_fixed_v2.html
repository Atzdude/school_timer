Help & guide
Adding a Timer
1
Type the exam name, e.g. "Mathematics Paper 1".
2
Type the duration: 2h, 1h30m, 2 hours, 90 (minutes) or 1:30 (hours:minutes). Or click a Quick fill chip.
3
Click Add exam or press Enter. The duration stays filled in, so adding several exams of the same length is quick.
Bulk Adding
1
Click Add from a list.
2
Paste one exam per line, with the duration after the last comma: English Language, Paper 1, 2h. Lines without a duration use the default shown in the dialog.
Running the exam
1
Click Present (or press P) to show the hall view on the projector.
2
Start Reading time if needed, then Start all (or press Space).
3
Turn on Lock so a stray key press can't pause the exams.
Saving Sessions
1
Click Save current to store today's exams under a name. Click the session later to load it.
Tip: Timers are saved automatically. If the computer restarts, reopen the app and running exams continue from their real end time.
Header
Reading TimeFull-screen countdown before writing starts. Pick 5, 10 or 15 minutes next to it.
PresentPresentation mode: full-screen hall view sized for the back row. Numbers grow to fill the screen. Press P or Esc to leave.
LockHides editing controls and turns off keyboard shortcuts so nothing changes by accident. Click again to unlock.
All exams
Start AllStarts every exam that still has time left.
Pause allPauses running exams. Paused cards show a yellow PAUSED tag and dimmed numbers.
Reset allStops every exam and puts it back to its full duration.
Keep finishedOn: finished exams move to a shelf below. Off: they disappear 30 seconds after time is up.
Clear allDeletes every exam. This can't be undone.
Card sizeChanges card size while setting up. In the hall view it makes exam names smaller or larger; the numbers always fill the space left.
